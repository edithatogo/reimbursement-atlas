# Specification

Accept the owner's explicit assertion of raw PBS redistribution permission and
apply it once at source level. Do not require a publisher document or repeated
checksum approvals. Record the basis truthfully as owner attestation, not as an
independently verified publisher grant or an invented open licence.

The PBS provenance, archive verification and variant acquisition surfaces must
derive permission from the same record. Non-PBS sources remain outside scope;
missing, malformed or revoked records cannot approve redistribution. Preserve
raw copyright notices, attribution, source identity and checksums. Keep Apache-2.0
software licensing separate. No raw payloads enter Git. Permission does not
create missing bytes or imply external publication, research claims or papers.
