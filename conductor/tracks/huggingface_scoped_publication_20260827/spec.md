# Specification

## Goal

Refresh the stale Hugging Face dataset and Space with current medallion metadata without treating
bounded metadata publication as a paper, policy claim, or unrestricted research publication.

## Acceptance criteria

- Metadata mutation requires repository and evidence readiness, complete protocols, passing source
  contracts and data quality, public-data policy, and permissive rights for every staged artifact.
- Full research publication continues to require research and policy-claim readiness.
- Non-staged historical candidates cannot block metadata deployment and cannot be copied remotely.
- Raw payloads, restricted descriptors, papers, and preprints remain excluded.
- Exact-head hosted checks pass before mutation.
