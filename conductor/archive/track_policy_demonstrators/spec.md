# First policy demonstrators

## Scope

Produce reproducible, descriptive demonstrator briefs for genomics/pathology
coverage-price linkage, cognitive/procedural schedule relativities and medicine
public-amount missingness. Demonstrators use permitted derived records or
clearly labelled fixtures and retain source, licence and transformation
boundaries.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each demonstrator has a stable identifier, typed inputs, reproducible output
  and documented limitation set.
- Genomics/pathology outputs distinguish published amounts, coverage metadata
  and utilisation; they do not infer causal effects or universal eligibility.
- Cognitive/procedural outputs report selected schedule relativities only; they
  do not infer reward, value, incentives or clinician income.
- Medicine outputs describe public-amount missingness only; they do not infer
  confidential rebates, net prices or patient cost exposure.
- Claim packages remain pending accountable review where required, and papers
  and preprints remain excluded.
