# Specification

## Goal

Record the authoritative PBS format transition, preserve the distinction between schedule
publications and current API/CSV distribution, and qualify the Services Australia PBS Item Report
as a separate aggregate utilisation source.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The December 2025 PBS notice records the API release, monthly cadence, CSV alternative and
   XML/text retirement from 1 May 2026.
2. The archive landing page and 2007 page remain deterministic discovery inputs.
3. The Magda record is traced to its authoritative data.gov.au CKAN package.
4. All eight CKAN resources are signature-validated in ignored storage with path-free SHA-256
   receipts and CC BY 3.0 AU attribution metadata.
5. Documentation does not conflate processed-claims utilisation with schedule price, prescribing,
   supply, net-price or complete coverage evidence.
6. Tests, source-policy and deterministic-generation gates pass.
