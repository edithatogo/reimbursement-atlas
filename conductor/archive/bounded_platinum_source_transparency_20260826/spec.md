# Bounded Source-Transparency Platinum Promotion

## Overview

Operationally validate Gold-to-Platinum promotion by releasing the existing
source-transparency dashboard within its approved metadata-only scope.

## Requirements

- Promote the source registry to Silver as a source-faithful typed metadata artifact.
- Promote the current approved source-transparency claim package to Gold.
- Validate the product contract against current file hashes and accountable review.
- Require repository release readiness, public-data policy, product rights, Gold input,
  and current scoped claim review before Platinum approval.
- Keep unrelated Platinum candidates and global evidence/publication readiness independent.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Exactly one Platinum product is approved within scope.
- Its promotion decision identifies every required and passed gate.
- Changed contract inputs fail closed until the contract is refreshed and reviewed.
- Unsupported research, accessibility, DOI, archive, paper and preprint claims remain prohibited.
- Local and hosted quality, browser, security, readiness and deterministic-generation gates pass.

## External Gates

No external mutation is required. This contract records repository product promotion only.

## Out of Scope

- Dataset, archive, DOI, paper or preprint publication.
- General evidence-readiness or policy-readiness approval.
- Promotion of unrelated software-package or archive candidates.
